# Video 5 — A UPI that is not theirs

Length: about 1 minute 15 seconds.
New feature: stop tracking one UPI id on a friend or family card, and scroll the ledger inside the page.

Follow `00-shoot-rules.md`.

Prep, off camera: one friend or family card that shows two UPI chips, and one of those ids is the wrong person. If you only have one id, add a second match off camera, then roll. Office cards do not have the remove control. Use Friends or Family.

The voice never reads the id. Blur or crop the id string in the edit if it is sensitive. On the shoot, the × is the subject.

## 0:00 — The card

Open **People**. Scroll to the person. Hold the card: name, payment count, and the UPI chips.

Voice:

> This person is tracked from the UPI id on their payments. Sometimes a second id lands on the same card, and it is not them.

## 0:12 — The wrong chip

Move the cursor to the × on the mismatched chip. The button's name is "Stop tracking … as {name}". Do not read that full label aloud if it contains the id.

Voice:

> I can drop that one id. The person stays. Their other payments stay.

## 0:22 — Remove it

Click the ×. Wait until the chip is gone. A line appears near the top of the page: "{id} is no longer tracked as {name}." If payments were unlabeled, the line says how many.

Hold the message, then the card without that chip.

Voice:

> That id is no longer this person. Payments that only matched it are unlabeled, so I can file them correctly. I did not delete the bank rows.

Do not click **Remove** on the whole person. That clears the person, which is a different action. If a confirm dialog appears, you clicked the wrong control. Cancel and reshoot from 0:12.

## 0:42 — The ledger scrolls in place

Click **Transactions**.

Frame the filters (From, To, Min, Max) and the top of the table together.

Scroll **inside** the transaction list, not the browser window. The filters and the day headings should stay understandable while rows move. Scroll for about 4 seconds, then stop on a day heading.

Voice:

> The list scrolls inside the page. The filters stay put, so a long month does not shove the controls off the screen.

End on the day heading you stopped on.

## Edit

- Cut the People message a frame after it finishes appearing.
- If the id in the message is too revealing, cover that sentence with the voice and crop the message, or trim to the card after the chip disappears.
