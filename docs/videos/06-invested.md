# Video 6 — Invested is not spent

Length: about 1 minute 10 seconds.
New feature: money categorized as Investments is left out of expenditure, and Overview has an Invested tab.

Follow `00-shoot-rules.md`.

Prep: the month on screen needs at least one debit whose category is **Investments** (a broker, a SIP, a mutual fund). If the month has none, correct one row off camera: Transactions, set **Where it went** to Investments, then start the take. Do not do that correction inside this clip unless you want a longer version. The short version starts with it already labeled.

## 0:00 — The spent line

Open **Overview**. The **Spending** chip is active.

Hold the line under the month title. It reads spent, invested, and received.

Voice:

> A SIP is money I moved, not money I spent on the month. The spent figure leaves it out. The invested figure is right beside it.

## 0:14 — Spending stays a spending picture

Scroll just enough to show the top of the daily chart, then scroll back to the tabs. Do not linger.

Voice:

> The charts on Spending are expenditure. The investment does not fatten a Tuesday bar.

## 0:26 — The other tab

Click **Invested**.

Hold the card: the kicker "Invested", the large amount, and the line "Moved into investments. Not counted as expenditure."

Voice:

> Invested is its own view. Same month. The total is what moved into investments. It is not in the expenditure number.

## 0:42 — The transfers

If the list under the total has rows, scroll slowly down that list. Each row is a date, the merchant or narration, and the amount.

Hold the first row for 2 seconds. Do not read an account number or a UPI id. Say the merchant name only if it is a broker or fund name you are comfortable saying.

Voice:

> Each line is one of those transfers. Date, where it went, amount. The month can show both lives of the money: what I spent, and what I set aside.

## 0:58 — Back

Click **Spending**. Hold the spent line again until the clip ends.

Voice:

> Spending is the month I lived. Invested is the month I set aside. They no longer share one total.

## If the Invested tab is empty

The card says "Nothing invested in {month}." That is the wrong month. Switch months off camera, or label one investment off camera, and reshoot from 0:00. Do not explain an empty state as the feature.
