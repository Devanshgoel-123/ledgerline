# Video 2 — Only your share counts

Length: about 1 minute 20 seconds.
New feature: split a bill with friends, including someone already saved on People.

Follow `00-shoot-rules.md`.

Prep, off camera: People already has at least one friend or family name. Transactions has one debit you are willing to split, ideally a dinner or a shared bill, not rent. Know the amounts before you roll. Example: bill ₹1,200, friend ₹700, you keep ₹500. Use your real numbers in the voice.

## 0:00 — The whole bill

Open **Transactions**. Scroll to the chosen row. The **Split** button is under the merchant name.

Hold the amount.

Voice:

> This left the account as one payment. It was not all mine.

## 0:08 — Open the split

Click **Split**.

The form opens under the merchant. If People has friends or family, a **Saved friend** menu appears: "Add someone you already track".

Voice:

> Split sits on the row. I do not retype the person if I already track them.

## 0:18 — Pick the person

Open the **Saved friend** menu. Click one name. The name fills the friend field.

Hold so the name is readable. Do not type a UPI id.

Voice:

> That name comes from People. Friends and family. Not a new contact book.

## 0:28 — Their share

Click the amount field next to that name. Type their share. Pause with both fields filled, before save.

If you need a second person, click **Add friend** and type the second name and amount. Otherwise skip that click.

Voice:

> Their share is the part that is not mine. The row still shows the full amount the bank took.

## 0:42 — Save

Click **Save split**. Wait until the form closes.

The quiet line should read: "Your share ₹… of ₹…" and the friend's name and amount.

Hold that line for 3 seconds. Do not click **Edit split**.

Voice:

> Saved. The ledger keeps the bank amount, and it remembers my share. Charts and the month total use my share. Their part is not my spending.

## 1:02 — The month agrees

Click **Overview**. Hold the spent figure in the line under the month title.

Voice:

> Overview spends my share, not the whole bill. A dinner with friends no longer looks like I paid for everyone.

End on that line.

## If the saved-friend menu is missing

People has no friend or family yet. Stop. Add one person off camera, then reshoot from 0:08. Do not invent the menu on camera.
