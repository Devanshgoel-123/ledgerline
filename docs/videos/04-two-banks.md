# Video 4 — Two banks, one scan

Length: about 1 minute.
New feature: Import can track two banks, and it shows the month this scan covers.

Follow `00-shoot-rules.md`.

Prep: Gmail already connected, so the button reads **Scan bank mail**, not **Connect Gmail and scan**. Know which two bank chips you will select. Logos appear only on the selected chips.

Do not click **Scan bank mail**. Do not click **Clear imported data**. This clip is the choice, not a live scan.

## 0:00 — The window

Open **Import**.

Hold the page subtitle under the Import heading. It names the scan window, newest mail first, and says Overview uses the same month.

Voice:

> Import tells you which days this scan covers before you run it. Newest mail first. Overview is that same month.

## 0:12 — One bank

Hold the **Banks** row. One chip is already selected and shows its logo.

Voice:

> One bank is the usual case. The logo marks the one that will be read.

## 0:20 — A second bank

Click a second bank chip. Its logo appears. The line under the chips reads: "Choose up to two banks. The logo shows on the ones you select."

Hold both selected chips.

Voice:

> A second account can come along. Two banks in one scan. Not the whole inbox. Only the alert senders for the banks you picked.

## 0:36 — The cap

Hover a third chip. It should look unavailable while two are selected. Do not force a click if it is disabled.

Voice:

> A third one waits. Two is the cap, so a scan stays on the accounts you meant.

## 0:46 — What scan would do

Move the cursor to **Scan bank mail** and stop. Do not click.

Point at the Bank mail header, then the line under it: "In your bank app, turn on email alerts for transactions. If those mails are off, a scan has nothing to import."

Voice:

> Scan reads debit and credit alerts from those two banks. The amount is stored. Gmail stays read-only. Those alerts have to be turned on in the bank app. If the mail is off, this scan has nothing to import. I am not starting it in this clip.

Click the second bank again so you leave the account on the banks you actually use. Hold the restored selection for 1 second, then end.

## If only one bank preset exists

Say the two-bank line anyway while showing the single chip and the "up to two" sentence. Do not fake a second logo.
