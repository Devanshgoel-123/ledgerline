# Video 3 — A payment the bank never saw

Length: about 55 seconds.
New feature: add an expense by hand.

Follow `00-shoot-rules.md`.

Prep: stay on a month you can add one small row to. Example you will actually type: description "Dinner", amount a round number, category Food or Outing, today's date or a date inside the selected month. Say those real values in the voice.

## 0:00 — The gap

Open **Transactions**. The **Add expense** button sits above the list.

Voice:

> Some money never hits this account. Cash. A Splitwise share. A bill someone else paid that is still mine. This row does not wait for a bank mail alert.

## 0:08 — The form

Click **Add expense**.

The form shows What, Amount, Category, Date.

Voice:

> Add expense writes one row. It is labeled manual. It is not a fake bank alert.

## 0:16 — Fill it

Click **What**. Type a short description. Example placeholder on screen is "Dinner, or a Splitwise share". Use a real one-line description.

Click **Amount**. Type the number.

Open **Category**. Pick the matching category. Leave Investments alone in this clip.

Set **Date** to a day in the month on screen.

Hold the filled form for 2 seconds. Do not submit yet.

Voice:

> What it was, how much, which part of life, which day. That is enough for it to land in the month.

## 0:36 — Save

Click **Save**. Wait until the form closes and the new row is in the list.

Scroll the list only if the new row is off screen. Hold the new merchant name and amount.

Voice:

> It is in the ledger. The category total and the month include it. I did not import a statement to record it.

End on the new row.

## Do not

- Do not click **Delete** on the new row during the take.
- Do not split this row in the same clip. That is video 2.

## Edit

- One continuous take if the typing is clean. Cut the typing shorter in the edit if it drags, and keep the filled form and the saved row.
